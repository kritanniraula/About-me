Biography

Hi, I'm Kritan Niraula, a 20-year-old BCA student from Kathmandu, Nepal. I am currently studying at Kathford International College. I have a strong interest in technology, programming, and computer science. I enjoy learning new things and developing my skills through academic projects and practical experiences.

Hobbies and Interests

In my free time, I enjoy playing and watching cricket, exploring new places, hiking, walking, and learning about new technologies. I also enjoy working on programming projects and improving my knowledge of web development, databases, and software development.

One of my main interests is technology, especially data science and machine learning. I enjoy solving problems and learning how technology can be used to solve real-world problems. I am continuously working on improving my programming and analytical skills.

Personal Values and Beliefs

I strongly believe in hard work, discipline, continuous learning, and respecting others. I believe that mistakes are an important part of learning and that consistent effort can help achieve long-term goals. I also value honesty, kindness, and maintaining a positive attitude toward challenges.

What Makes Me Unique

What sets me apart is my curiosity and willingness to learn new things. I enjoy exploring different areas of technology and challenging myself to improve my skills. I like combining my academic knowledge with practical projects and experiences. I believe that being curious and adaptable helps me grow both personally and professionally.

Goals and Aspirations

Looking ahead, my primary career goal is to become a data scientist and build a career in data science and machine learning. I want to develop strong programming, analytical, and problem-solving skills and work on meaningful projects that can have a positive impact. I also hope to continue learning new technologies and become a successful professional in the technology field.

Family

My parents, Basu Dev Niraula and Gita Niraula, have played an important role in my life. They have always supported me in my education and personal growth. Their guidance, encouragement, and support have helped me become the person I am today and motivated me to work hard toward my goals.

Stories and Memories

Some of my fondest memories are the moments I have spent with my family. Spending time together during festivals, family gatherings, and special occasions has created many memorable experiences. These moments have taught me the importance of family, togetherness, and supporting one another.

Values and Lessons

My family has taught me the importance of education, hard work, respect, responsibility, and kindness. From a young age, I have been encouraged to work hard, learn from my mistakes, respect others, and never stop improving myself. These values continue to guide me in my studies and personal life.

Their Influence on Me

My family's support has been instrumental in shaping my goals and interests. Their encouragement has motivated me to pursue my education and develop a career in the technology field. Their guidance has also taught me to remain responsible, disciplined, and focused on my goals while continuing to learn and improve.
